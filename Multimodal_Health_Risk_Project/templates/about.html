About HealthRisk AI

HealthRisk AI is a multimodal healthcare prediction system designed to analyze multiple types of health information and provide disease-specific risk assessment with Explainable Artificial Intelligence.

Project Objective

The system integrates Laboratory Reports, Medical History, Vital Signs and Medical Imaging to support disease risk prediction and transparent healthcare analysis.

Target Diseases

  • Diabetes
  • Heart Disease
  • Bronchitis
  • Breast Cancer

Explainable AI

SHAP and LIME are used to identify important factors that influence the machine-learning prediction, making the system easier to interpret.

Risk Assessment

The system provides disease-specific prediction, risk-level classification, model confidence, contributing factors and preventive guidance.

Multimodal Data

  • Laboratory Reports
  • Medical History
  • Vital Signs
  • Medical Imaging

Technologies

  • Python
  • Flask
  • Scikit-learn
  • Pandas
  • SHAP / LIME
  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript
